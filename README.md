## 🧩 What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔗 **Learns your brand from one link** | Enter your website or Instagram handle and MultiCast builds your Brand Kit: logo, colors, fonts, voice, and audience. Every piece of content starts there, so nothing drifts off-tone. |
| 🪝 **Makes content that hooks** | Carousels, short videos, feed images, and text posts designed to stop the scroll — no prompts to write, no design tools to learn. |
| 🗓️ **Creates and runs campaigns** | Give it a theme and it makes weeks of content, shapes it into a campaign, and lines it up on a single calendar. |
| 📣 **Publishes to every channel** | The same content is fitted to each channel's format and goes out at the time you set. |
| 💬 **Handles comments and DMs** | Set a keyword on a post and everyone who comments it gets an automatic DM. AI replies answer from your brand knowledge, and hand off to you when they're not sure. Available on Instagram. |
| 📈 **Shows what's working** | Results at a glance: which formats, times, and posts perform, and what to make next. |
